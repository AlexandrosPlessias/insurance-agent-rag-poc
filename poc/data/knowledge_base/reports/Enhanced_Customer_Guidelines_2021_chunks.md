## **1. Refund Policy**

Customer data shared verbally during an interaction must be limited to what is strictly necessary for completing the current transaction or resolving the current issue. Employees must not repeat or confirm sensitive information (such as full card numbers, home addresses, or account passwords) in a voice loud enough to be overheard by other customers or non-authorized staff. An annual data protection awareness briefing is introduced for all customer-facing staff. This mandatory session covers the basics of data handling responsibilities, common mistakes to avoid, and the consequences of data misuse. Attendance is tracked and reported to HR. Violations of the new data access protocols are taken seriously and may result in a formal written warning for the first offense. Repeated violations or deliberate misuse of customer data will be subject to the organization's full disciplinary process.
