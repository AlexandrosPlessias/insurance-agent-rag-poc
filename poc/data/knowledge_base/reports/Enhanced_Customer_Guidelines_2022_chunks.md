## **1. Refund Policy**

The organization continues to expand its customer-friendly refund practices in line with industry benchmarks and customer satisfaction data. The refund window has been extended to 21 days from the date of purchase, providing a more generous timeframe that reflects the reality that many customers do not have immediate opportunity to evaluate a product or identify a concern within the first week or two of ownership.
