## **5. Data Protection & Privacy**

A zerotrust access model is now in effect: every data access request is verified in real-time regardless of the employee's role, seniority, or previous access history. The system evaluates each access attempt against the employee's current role permissions, the business context of the request, and behavioral pattern analysis. Anomalous access patterns trigger immediate alerts to the security team. Automated breach detection systems monitor data flows continuously. In the event of a confirmed or suspected data breach, an automated 24hour notification protocol is triggered to inform all potentially affected customers, providing them with clear information about what data may have been compromised, what the organization is doing in response, and what steps the customer should take to protect themselves. Data protection certification is required annually for all employees, consisting of an online training module followed by a formal assessment. Employees who do not complete certification by the annual deadline have their system access automatically revoked until certification is achieved.
