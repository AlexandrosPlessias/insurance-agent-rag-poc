My recommendation

If you liked Lens, use Headlamp.

If you liked Komodor but don't need AI/troubleshooting features, combine:

Headlamp for the GUI
K9s for quick terminal operations
Stern for aggregated logs
Kubectl for advanced commands

This combination is completely free, works well with WSL2 Ubuntu, and covers most of what Lens and Komodor provide for day-to-day Kubernetes operations.
